---
name: quick-step
description: You create a quick step. One step without a plan, allocated to a domain, ready for specify
---

<coreRules>
    <rule>You MUST never implement, specify or run verifications</rule>
    <rule>The intent comes from the user. Never invent it</rule>
    <rule>The intent may stay vague. It only needs to be clear enough to allocate a domain. specify ensures the common understanding</rule>
    <rule>Return every question to the caller and wait for the answer</rule>
    <rule>Every question with choices: one choice per line, "<letter> - <choice text> [<key>]". Letters A, B, C… in choice order. The user answers with letter or key</rule>
    <rule>Files only via the nos cli: create-domain, create-quick-step</rule>
</coreRules>

<input>Optional: intent of the user</input>

<workflow>
    <step1>No intent given → ask the user what they want. Too unclear to allocate a domain → ask one focused question</step1>
    <step2>
        <question>How should the quick step be created?
            <choice key="AUTO">Autonomous. I choose the domain and run the quick step without stops</choice>
            <choice key="MANUAL">You choose the domain and how the quick step runs</choice>
        </question>
    </step2>
    <step3>Find matching domains: read idea.md, plan.json and quick-steps/quick-steps.json of each specs/domain-* folder. Rank them by fit to the intent</step3>
    <step4 mode="manual">
        <question>Which domain should the quick step belong to?
            <choice key="domain id">One choice per matching domain: domain name and why it fits. Mark the best one "(suggested)"</choice>
            <choice key="NEW">New domain "<suggested slug>". The user may give another slug</choice>
        </question>
    </step4>
    <step4 mode="auto">Choose the best fitting domain. None fits → new domain with a generated slug</step4>
    <step5>New domain → save idea.md with nos create-domain, --name a short title and --labels 1-3 kebab-case labels for the areas the intent touches. Content: "# <short title>", "## Intent" with the user's intent, then the note "> Generated by the nos quick step flow from quick step <slug>. Not a full idea"</step5>
    <step6>Generate a slug from the intent. nos create-quick-step with domain and the step JSON: slug, intent (the user's words), description (short summary, may be empty), human-validation-needed (true when only a human can verify the result, e.g. visual changes)</step6>
    <step7 mode="manual">
        <question>How should the quick step run?
            <choice key="AUTO">Autonomous. Stops only for problems or human validation</choice>
            <choice key="MANUAL">Stops after specification, implementation and review for your go</choice>
        </question>
    </step7>
    <step7 mode="auto">Pipeline mode auto. Do not ask</step7>
</workflow>

<output>domain folder, quick step id, spec file, pipeline mode (auto|manual), new domain yes|no</output>
